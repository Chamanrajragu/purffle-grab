How to download YouTube videos and MP3s

The same three steps work for TikTok, Instagram, SoundCloud and every other supported site.

1. Copy the link

Copy a video, Short, playlist or channel URL from your browser. PurffleGrab notices a copied link and offers to grab it — or press Ctrl+Shift+V to download it in one step.

2. Paste and analyse

Paste one link or many and press Analyze. For playlists and channels you'll see every item, ready to tick or untick.

3. Pick a format and download

MP4 up to 4K, MP3 up to 320 kbps, FLAC, M4A and more — or tap a preset. Files land in your Music › PurffleGrab folder.

Frequently asked questions

Is PurffleGrab free?
Yes. PurffleGrab is free to download and use — no ads, no account, no subscription, no download limits.
Which sites can PurffleGrab download from?
YouTube, Spotify, TikTok, Instagram, SoundCloud, X (Twitter), Facebook, Vimeo, Reddit, Twitch, Bandcamp, Dailymotion, Bilibili, Mixcloud and well over a thousand more — any site the bundled yt-dlp engine supports. Paste a link and PurffleGrab tells you what it found.
How do I download a Spotify playlist to MP3?
Copy the playlist link in Spotify (Share → Copy link), paste it into PurffleGrab and press Analyze. Pick Audio → MP3 (or the MP3 320 preset), untick any tracks you don't want, and press Download. Each track is matched to the best available audio and saved with its title, artist, album and cover art.
Can I download a whole Spotify playlist, even a big one?
Out of the box PurffleGrab reads up to the first 100 tracks of a playlist — a limit of Spotify's public data. Add a free Spotify developer key in Settings and your own playlists download in full, however long. Spotify's own editorial playlists stay capped at 100 because Spotify blocks API access to them.
Can PurffleGrab keep a playlist in sync and download only new songs?
Yes. Grab a Spotify or YouTube playlist into the same folder again and PurffleGrab downloads only the tracks added since — songs already there are skipped without being searched or fetched again. Schedule the playlist daily or weekly in the Scheduler and the folder stays mirrored to it. Delete a song and the next sync brings it back; turn the behaviour off in Settings if you want full re-downloads.
Can I just type a song name instead of a link?
Yes. Type “Artist - Song” in the box, or paste a whole tracklist with one song per line, and PurffleGrab finds the best-matching upload for each — preferring official audio over live versions, covers and sped-up edits.
Can it download YouTube videos in 4K?
Yes. Choose the Best video (4K) preset or set the quality to 2160p. Earlier versions quietly fell back to 1080p for 4K requests; since v12 you get the real 4K or 1440p stream, merged into an MP4.
Can I download TikTok videos and Instagram Reels?
Yes — paste the TikTok, Instagram post or Reel link and download it as MP4, or as MP3 for the audio. Some Instagram posts need you to be logged in: turn on “Use browser cookies” in Settings and PurffleGrab reads your existing browser login locally.
How do I fix “Sign in to confirm you’re not a bot”?
YouTube sometimes asks for this when it sees many requests. PurffleGrab tells you when it happens. Waiting a few minutes usually clears it; for a lasting fix, open Settings → Downloads and choose the browser you're signed in to YouTube with under “Use browser cookies” (Firefox works most reliably).
What happens when YouTube changes and downloads stop working?
PurffleGrab keeps its download engine (yt-dlp) up to date by itself — it checks daily, and if a download fails in a way that points to an outdated engine it fetches a fresh one and retries automatically.
Is the FLAC download really lossless?
FLAC is a lossless format, but YouTube and Spotify only stream compressed audio, so a FLAC made from them is a perfect copy of that compressed source — not CD quality. For the smallest files with no audible difference, MP3 320 or M4A is the better choice. The app says this right where you pick the format.
Do I need to install FFmpeg or Python?
No. FFmpeg and the yt-dlp engine are bundled inside PurffleGrab. Install it and it works.
Where are my downloads saved?
In Music › PurffleGrab by default. Single downloads go straight in; a playlist or album gets its own folder named after it. You can change the folder in Settings.
Does PurffleGrab work on Mac and Linux?
Yes. There's a Windows installer (Windows 10 and 11), macOS DMGs for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs (macOS 13 Ventura or newer), and Linux AppImage and .deb packages. PurffleGrab isn't notarised by Apple yet, so the first time you open it, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
Is PurffleGrab safe? Does it collect data?
PurffleGrab has no telemetry, analytics, trackers or ads. It only talks to the sites whose links you paste (plus GitHub, to check for updates). Downloads, history and settings stay on your computer.
Is PurffleGrab open source?
No. PurffleGrab is free to use (freeware), distributed as ready-to-run installers under an end-user licence (versions up to 9.0 were MIT-licensed). The engines it bundles — yt-dlp and FFmpeg — are open source, and their licences are included.
