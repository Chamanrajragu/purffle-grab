Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PurffleGrab.

Is PurffleGrab really free?
Yes, 100% free and open-source under the MIT license. No hidden costs, no premium version, no subscriptions, no ads. It will always be free.
What audio and video formats does PurffleGrab support?
Audio: MP3, M4A (AAC), FLAC (lossless), WAV, Opus, OGG Vorbis, AAC. Video: MP4 (up to 4K 2160p), MKV, WebM. Audio bitrates from 64 to 320 kbps. Video quality from 360p to 4K.
Can I download entire Spotify playlists?
Yes! Paste any Spotify playlist, album, or track link. PurffleGrab reads the track list, finds the best audio match on YouTube, downloads it, and embeds Spotify metadata including cover art, artist, album, and title.
Does PurffleGrab work on Windows 11?
Yes, PurffleGrab works on both Windows 10 and Windows 11 (64-bit). It comes as a standard installer with all dependencies (yt-dlp, FFmpeg) bundled — no extra software required.
Is PurffleGrab safe? Does it contain malware?
PurffleGrab is completely open-source on GitHub — anyone can inspect every line of code. It contains zero telemetry, analytics, ads, or trackers. It bundles yt-dlp and FFmpeg, which are widely-used, trusted open-source tools. No data ever leaves your computer.
How do I update the download engine?
Go to Settings → Downloader engine → Click "Update engine." This updates the bundled yt-dlp to the latest version, ensuring maximum compatibility with YouTube and other supported sites.
Can I download YouTube videos in 4K?
Yes! Select the "Best video (4K)" preset or manually set the resolution to 2160p in the options panel. PurffleGrab will download the highest quality available and mux it into an MP4 file.
How does the format converter work?
Go to the Convert tab, drag and drop any audio or video file (or click to browse), select the target format (MP3, FLAC, M4A, WAV, MP4, WebM, MKV), choose quality, and click Convert. The converted file downloads instantly.
What is the download scheduler?
The scheduler lets you set a date and time for downloads to start automatically. You can schedule one-time or recurring (daily/weekly) downloads. Useful for grabbing content during off-peak hours or automating regular downloads.
Does PurffleGrab support subtitles?
Yes, you can download and embed subtitles in any available language. It supports both manually uploaded and auto-generated YouTube captions, and can embed them directly into the video file as soft subtitles.
What is SponsorBlock?
SponsorBlock is a community-driven database of sponsor segments in YouTube videos. When enabled in PurffleGrab, it automatically removes sponsor reads, intros, outros, and self-promotions from downloaded videos.
Is downloading from YouTube and Spotify legal?
PurffleGrab is a tool for personal use. Downloading copyrighted content may violate the Terms of Service of Spotify/YouTube and copyright law in your country. We encourage using PurffleGrab only for content you have the right to download (e.g., your own content, Creative Commons, public domain).
